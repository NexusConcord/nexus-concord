Industries & Deal Profile

Businesses Represented.

Nexus Concord represents owner-operators across service businesses, consumer-facing operations, and behavioral health practices in Indiana and the Midwest.

01

Service-Based Businesses

Professional services firms, B2B consulting, business services, and skilled trade services. The most common category among lower-middle-market sellers — and the deepest area of Nexus Concord's brokerage experience. Recurring revenue, customer concentration, and contract assignability are the value drivers buyers scrutinize most closely.

02

Retail & E-Commerce

Established retail operations, e-commerce businesses, direct-to-consumer brands, and multichannel sellers. These transactions require careful navigation of inventory normalization, customer concentration, supplier relationships, and platform dependency considerations.

03

Hospitality & Food Service

Restaurants, hospitality operations, food service businesses, and consumer experience operations. Owner involvement, lease structures, staffing models, and operational continuity are the value drivers buyers scrutinize most closely during diligence.

04

Behavioral Health & ABA Practices

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) practices and adjacent behavioral health operations. A growing area of private equity consolidation, where clinician-owners are often poorly served by generalist brokers who don't understand the regulatory, accreditation, and payer landscape. Nexus Concord brings sector-specific awareness to these transactions.

Owner Readiness

Are you ready to sell?

Most owners Nexus Concord speaks with aren't ready to go to market today — they're three months, twelve months, or two years out. The work done before listing has the largest impact on final sale price. The earlier that work begins, the better the outcome.

Common readiness questions:

  • → Are your financials clean enough to survive due diligence?
  • → Could the business operate for 90 days without you?
  • → Is your largest customer less than 25% of revenue?
  • → Are key contracts assignable in a sale?
  • → Have you and your family aligned on what comes after?
