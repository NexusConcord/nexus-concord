Industries & Deal Profile

Who we represent.

Nexus Concord focuses on Indiana and Midwest lower middle market businesses across construction, government contracting, services, and supply chain.

01

Construction & Specialty Trades

General contractors, specialty trade contractors, and construction services businesses. Indiana's construction market combines stable demand with significant fragmentation — creating opportunities for disciplined sellers.

02

Government Contracting

Federal, state, and local government contractors, including SDVOSB, WOSB, and 8(a) certified businesses. Specialized buyer pools value contract vehicles, past performance, and security clearances — and pricing reflects that value when the firm is positioned correctly.

03

Business & Professional Services

Recurring-revenue services businesses, B2B consulting, professional services firms, and managed services providers. Recurring revenue and customer concentration drive these valuations — both areas where preparation work translates directly to multiple expansion.

04

Supply Chain & Distribution

Distribution, logistics, and supply chain businesses serving the Midwest industrial corridor. Nexus Concord understands how to position these firms to strategic acquirers and platform-building private equity buyers.

Owner Readiness

Are you ready to sell?

Most owners we speak with aren't ready to go to market today — they're three months, twelve months, or two years out. The work done before listing has the largest impact on final sale price. The earlier we start that work together, the better the outcome.

Common readiness questions:

  • → Are your financials clean enough to survive due diligence?
  • → Could the business operate for 90 days without you?
  • → Is your largest customer less than 25% of revenue?
  • → Are key contracts assignable in a sale?
  • → Have you and your family aligned on what comes after?
