The Nexus Concord Process

Disciplined process. Principal-led.

Every engagement follows a deliberate sequence — built on institutional rigor, run at the scale and pace owner-operated businesses actually need.

A four-phase engagement.

From the first valuation conversation to the final closing wire, every Nexus Concord engagement moves through four deliberate phases. The same advisor leads each phase. The same standards apply throughout.

01
Phase One

Preparation & Analysis

Before any business goes to market, we do the work that determines whether the sale will succeed. This phase has three components, executed in parallel.

Pre-market diligence. We conduct our own structured diligence on the seller — financial recasting to true Seller's Discretionary Earnings, operational documentation, contract review, IP assignments, corporate governance, and known compliance risks. The work won't anticipate every individual buyer demand, but it surfaces foundational issues we can address on your terms, not during a buyer's renegotiation.

Valuation. We deliver a comprehensive Opinion of Value grounded in real comparable transactions — not an inflated number designed to win the listing.

Psychological preparation. We prepare you for the predictable inflection points where the post-LOI process tests sellers — buyer-induced doubt, fatigue-driven concessions, family alignment, identity decoupling. Pattern recognition applied to the human side of the transaction.

This phase typically takes 30 to 60 days and produces the foundation for everything that follows.

02
Phase Two

Marketing & Outreach

Confidential, professional, and targeted. We prepare a Confidential Information Memorandum and anonymous teaser, then conduct a controlled outreach to qualified buyers — strategic acquirers, private equity, family offices, and individual buyers with verified financial capacity.

All outreach is gated by signed Non-Disclosure Agreements and buyer financial qualification. Your confidentiality is protected at every step.

03
Phase Three

Buyer Management & LOI

Qualified buyer interest converts to management meetings, structured information exchange, and ultimately competing Indications of Interest and Letters of Intent. We manage the negotiation dynamics, surface and address concerns early, and structure the LOI to protect your interests through diligence.

Due diligence coordination begins here. We organize the data room, coordinate buyer requests, and prepare the operational, financial, and legal documentation buyers will scrutinize.

04
Phase Four

Deal Management & Closing

From signed LOI to closing wire, we manage the dozens of moving parts that determine whether a deal closes well, closes badly, or doesn't close at all. Coordination with your attorney on the definitive purchase agreement. Coordination with your CPA on tax structuring. Buyer financing oversight. Closing checklist management.

The job ends when the wire hits your account — and not a moment before.

Engagement Economics

Transparent. Aligned. Honest.

If the deal doesn't close, you owe nothing. That alignment is the entire point.
