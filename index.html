Double Lehman: 10% of the first $1M, 8% of the second $1M, 6% of the third $1M, 4% of the fourth $1M, 2% thereafter. Figures are illustrative and represent broker success fees only.

Why the gap exists. Most business brokerages operate as franchises. When a seller pays a 10% success fee, that fee funds three layers of overhead before it pays for the person actually representing them. First, corporate franchise obligations: royalty fees on gross revenues, brand and marketing fund contributions, technology fees, and the recoupment of an initial franchise fee paid to license the brand. Second, office-level overhead and profit for the local franchisee. Third, the split with the producing agent who actually runs the deal.

By the time all three layers are funded, less than half of the seller's fee typically reaches the person doing the work. The 10% isn't priced against the work performed — it's priced against the cost of running a franchise system.

Nexus Concord is not a franchise. There is no corporate parent taking royalties. There is no marketing fund absorbing a percentage of gross. There is no producing agent splitting the fee with a franchise office. The 5% success fee funds the seller's engagement directly — and the savings stay with the seller.

Strategic Preparation

Preparation is the difference between a listing and a sale.

The most successful business sales don't start with a listing — they start with a strategy. Of every factor an owner can control, the work done in the months before going to market has the largest impact on final sale price.

PHASE 01

Financial Preparedness

Clean financials are the bedrock of a successful sale. We guide you through preparing books for due diligence, recasting financials to true Seller's Discretionary Earnings, and organizing every document buyers and lenders will request.

Marketing & Buyer Targeting

A confidential, professional outreach to the buyers most likely to value your business — strategic acquirers, private equity, family offices, and qualified individual buyers. A decade of Indiana and Midwest market relationships, gated by signed NDAs and verified buyer capacity.

PHASE 03

Psychological Preparation

The work most brokers ignore. Nexus Concord prepares you for the predictable inflection points where buyers test sellers during diligence — induced doubt, fatigue-driven concessions, family alignment, identity decoupling. Pattern recognition applied to the human side of a transaction.

Our Philosophy

The center of agreement.

Nexus Concord's name is its philosophy. The firm exists to be the central point — the Nexus — where every party in a transaction can reach Concord: a fair and successful agreement.

A business sale is more than numbers. It's about managing people, expectations, and emotions. Buyers sometimes posture by withholding financials or manufacturing diligence issues, mistaking aggression for leverage. Sellers sometimes anchor to numbers that reflect what they need rather than what the market supports.

Nexus Concord's job is to dismantle those roadblocks. By managing information flow, enforcing process discipline, and de-escalating conflict before it threatens the deal, the firm creates the conditions where both sides move forward with confidence.
