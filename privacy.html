Privacy

Privacy policy.

Nexus Concord LLC respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect personal information you provide through this website.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: name, email, phone, company, revenue range, timeline, industry, and any message content. We may also collect basic analytics data (pages visited, time on site, referring source) through privacy-friendly analytics tools.

How We Use Information

Information you provide is used solely to respond to your inquiry, evaluate potential engagement, and communicate with you about our services. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Confidentiality

All information shared with Nexus Concord is held in strict confidence. Information about your business is never shared with potential buyers or third parties without your written authorization.

Data Retention

We retain inquiry information for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain professional records. You may request deletion of your information at any time by emailing reed@nexusconcord.com.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies, primarily for analytics. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies that follow you across other sites.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to reed@nexusconcord.com.
