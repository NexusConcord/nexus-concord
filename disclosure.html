Disclosure

Important disclosures.

Nature of Services

Nexus Concord LLC ("Nexus Concord") is a business intermediary and M&A advisory firm. Nexus Concord assists business owners with the marketing, negotiation preparation, and closing of business sales.

Not Legal Advice

Nexus Concord is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. While the firm's principal holds law degrees, no attorney-client relationship is formed through engagement with Nexus Concord. All clients are strongly encouraged to retain independent legal counsel for any transaction.

Not Tax or Investment Advice

Nexus Concord does not provide tax, accounting, or investment advice. Clients should retain qualified tax advisors and accountants for transaction-related tax planning and structuring.

Engagement Required

All advisory services are provided pursuant to a written engagement agreement. Nothing on this website constitutes an offer to provide services. Information presented here is for general informational purposes only.

Confidentiality

Nexus Concord operates under strict confidentiality standards. Information shared during initial consultations is held in confidence. However, no information transmitted via this website's contact form should be considered confidential until a formal engagement agreement is executed.

No Guarantees

Past performance does not guarantee future results. No representation is made regarding the likelihood of a successful sale, the price at which a business will sell, or the timing of any transaction. Every business sale involves substantial risk and uncertainty.

Jurisdiction

Nexus Concord LLC is organized under the laws of the State of Delaware. Services are primarily provided to clients in Indiana and surrounding Midwest states.
