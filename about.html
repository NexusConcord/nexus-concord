About Nexus Concord

Concord, defined.

A boutique M&A advisory firm built on the conviction that representing an owner well requires understanding far more than the deal itself.

Why This Firm Exists

A different premise.

The word concord means agreement reached through alignment — not compromise, not capitulation, but genuine accord between parties whose interests have been carefully understood.

That definition is the entire premise of this firm.

Most M&A transactions in the lower middle market are run on a model designed for someone else's benefit. Tiered commissions reward brokers for closing quickly rather than closing well. Engagement teams hand sellers off to junior staff after the pitch. Process becomes performance.

Nexus Concord was built to operate differently.

The Founder

Paul Reed

A decade of brokerage experience, a JD, an MBA, an LL.M. Candidacy in Corporate and Commercial Law, and the operational discipline of a Marine Corps veteran. The combination is uncommon in the lower middle market — and it shapes how every Nexus Concord engagement is run.

For ten years, I've represented business owners through the most consequential transaction of their careers. That work taught me what most brokers either don't see or refuse to acknowledge: a sale is rarely about the deal itself. It's about the owner's family, their employees, their next chapter, and the legacy they want to leave behind.

Formal education deepened that conviction. A Juris Doctor from Ohio Northern University Pettit College of Law, an MBA from Louisiana State University, and an LL.M. Candidacy in Corporate and Commercial Law from Indiana University McKinney School of Law give me a structural understanding of how transactions actually work — not as marketing exercises, but as legal and financial instruments with consequences that play out for years after closing.

Nexus Concord exists because the lower middle market deserves advisory work that takes those consequences seriously.

How We Think About the Work

Whole-picture advisory.

A business sale is not a transaction in isolation. It is the convergence of financial, legal, operational, and deeply personal considerations — each of which deserves attention from someone capable of seeing the whole picture.

Modern advisory work too often defaults to narrow, mechanical thinking: spreadsheets without context, process without judgment, speed without strategy. That approach produces deals. It rarely produces outcomes that owners feel good about a year later.

Nexus Concord works the other way. We start with the owner's situation in full — business, family, market timing, post-close intentions — and build the engagement around it. The 5% model exists because it removes the incentive distortions that pull advisors away from that work. The principal-led structure exists because the work cannot be delegated.

There is another reason the principal-led structure matters. A decade of brokerage work has produced pattern recognition that cannot be transferred to a producing agent — the sense of when a buyer's diligence question is legitimate versus a negotiating tactic, when a working capital calculation is being weaponized, when a representation is worth pushing back on and when it isn't. Deals in the lower middle market often turn on exactly these moments. Nexus Concord represents sellers with the judgment to recognize them and the experience to respond.

In practice, that means three things most brokers don't do: structured pre-market diligence on the seller before going to market, honest valuation grounded in real comparable transactions, and direct preparation for the psychological pressure points that test every owner during the sale process. Operational, structural, and human — each side of the transaction taken seriously.

Where We Work

Indiana and the Midwest.

Nexus Concord is headquartered in Indiana and primarily serves owners across the state and broader Midwest. Indiana's lower middle market is underserved by national investment banks and over-served by transactional brokers. Nexus Concord occupies the space between — the rigor of institutional advisory work, delivered at the scale and pace appropriate to owner-operated businesses.
